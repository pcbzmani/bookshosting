ABOUT ME

I notice. I question. I write.

Welcome to my little corner of the internet. I am Ramya, a writer who enjoys looking beneath ordinary experiences to discover what they reveal about human connection, emotion, and existence.

Writing in both Tamil and English allows me to bridge two rich literary traditions. Some pieces are reflective essays, some are poetic verses, and others begin simply as a quiet observation that refuses to fade away.
